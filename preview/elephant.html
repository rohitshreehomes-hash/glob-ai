How to trade it (course rules)

  • 1 · EYE — fast pair prints a trough below the zero line after a decline. A bottom is forming. Do not enter yet.
  • 2 · TUSK — EMA 5 crosses back above EMA 8, price reclaims EMA 21 and breaks the previous bar high → the entry, ideally with volume expansion and an RSI turn up from oversold.
  • 3 · TRUNK — slow pair crosses above zero while the 144/233 trunk turns up → trend-change confirmation; keep the runner while the trunk rises.
  • Stop — below the eye swing (or 0.3 ATR beyond it). T1 at 1R, T2 at 2R. Tops are the exact mirror.
  • Never trade the first 5 min of the open or the last 10 min before the close — the indicator blocks those bars itself.
  • Always confirm with price action: support/resistance, trendline, or a rejection candle. The elephant alone is a scanner, not a system.
